Privacy policy

Between Stops doesn’t collect any data about you. Everything you put in the app stays on your phone.

Effective 27 September 2026. Applies to the Between Stops apps for iPhone, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS, and to this website.

In short

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No servers. The app has no server and no code of its own that sends anything over the internet.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. No third-party SDKs for any of these.
  • No AI. Suggestions come from clear rules that run on your phone.
  • You decide what leaves the phone. Only a file you choose to export or send, and only where you send it.

What the app keeps, and where

Your tasks, notes, links, phone numbers, places, reminder times, sessions and settings are kept in a database inside the app’s private storage on your phone. The developer never receives them and cannot see them.

The app asks iOS to leave this data out of iCloud and computer backups, and on Android it turns off Google backup and device-to-device transfer. That keeps your list private, but it also means a lost or reset phone takes the list with it, unless you exported a backup file yourself (see below).

Permissions, all optional

Each permission is asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs it, and only after the app explains why. Everything else works without it.

  • Notifications: for the gentle “Is now a good time?” reminders at times you choose. They never contain your tasks.
  • Location: only for your saved places. It saves where you are as a place, picks the saved place you’re at when you start a session, and, if you turn on arrival prompts, lets the phone’s own geofencing notice when you arrive. Your location stays on the phone and is never uploaded by the app.
  • Calendar: only if you turn on “Fit sessions around your calendar”, which is off by default. The app looks at when your events start and end in the next few hours to suggest a session that fits. It doesn’t read titles, places, notes or attendees, and it keeps nothing.
  • Face ID, fingerprint or passcode: only if you turn on the app lock. The phone checks it’s you; the app only learns “yes” or “no”.

The app never asks for your contacts, microphone, camera, photo library or motion data.

Things you share into the app

  • Text and links shared from another app open a new task for you to save.
  • Pictures (such as a screenshot) you share into the app are saved again without location or camera details, kept in the app’s private storage with the task, and never included in backup files.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: when you add a task by voice, Apple handles the speech under your Siri settings. The app receives only the resulting text.

Widgets, Lock Screen and watch

  • Widgets show fixed text such as “Got a few minutes?” and “Your next thing”. They never show your tasks.
  • While you do a task, the Lock Screen can show “1 thing in progress” and a timer, never the task itself. You can turn this off.
  • A paired Apple Watch or Wear OS watch can show a few tasks that fit. Titles go to the watch only while the app lock is off; with it on, the watch shows a count only. They travel over the phone’s direct link to your watch, not through a server.

Backups and files you choose to send

  • Export a backup: creates a file with your data and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email, another app) is your choice, and that service’s privacy terms apply. The app warns you first.
  • Keep a backup in a folder: writes a backup file to a folder you pick. If that folder syncs (iCloud Drive, Google Drive), your phone uploads it under your account; the app itself uploads nothing.
  • Send tasks: puts only the tasks you tick into a small file for someone else. It contains no dates, places or history.

Links, calls and messages

Opening a link, calling or composing a message happens only when you tap it. The app hands over to your browser, phone or messages app, and you finish there.

Optional tips

Tips are paid through the App Store or Google Play under your store account. The developer never sees your name, card or account; the app stores only that a tip exists, so it can unlock the extras.

On Android, Google Play’s billing library, which tips need, adds internet access to the app and may send its own diagnostics (such as billing errors) to Google. The developer doesn’t receive or control them, and they contain nothing you wrote in the app. Google’s privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything the app keeps on the phone, including reminders, places, pictures and pending shares. Deleting the app does the same. Backup files you exported are yours and stay where you put them.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you email for support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and aren’t shared with anyone. Please don’t send the contents of your tasks unless they’re needed to explain a problem.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that means data leaves your phone in a new way would also be explained in the app before it takes effect.
