Common questions

How do I move my tasks to a new phone?

Settings → Export a backup on the old phone, then Import a backup on the new one. The app keeps its data out of phone backups, so this is the way to bring your list along.

I’m not getting reminders.

Check Settings → Reminder windows is on, and that notifications for Between Stops are allowed in your phone’s Settings. Reminders never arrive during your quiet hours.

Arrival prompts don’t appear.

They need reminders turned on, notifications allowed and location set to “Always”. The phone decides exactly when you’ve arrived, so a prompt can come a little late.

Is there anything to buy?

No. Between Stops has no purchases, subscriptions or ads, and every feature is included.
