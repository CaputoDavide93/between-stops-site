A little time for the things you’ve put off.

Between Stops is a private iPhone and Android app. Say how many minutes you have and where you are, and it shows the small things you’ve been meaning to do that fit.

  • Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking, no AI.
  • Free, with an optional one-off tip. Nothing in the free app is limited.
  • Gentle by design: no streaks, no guilt.
